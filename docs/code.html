Donor Clarifications

When and how will I receive my 80G Tax Certificate? expand_more

Immediately following transaction settlement, your digitally signed 80G tax receipt is emailed to your registered address and dispatched via WhatsApp. In addition, an annual Form 10BE consolidated certificate is filed with the Income Tax Department at the end of the financial year.

Can I donate using UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM)? expand_more

Yes. Our payment gateway seamlessly supports all UPI applications, direct QR scan, NetBanking across all major public and private banks, and Indian debit/credit cards.

How can I audit how my donation was utilized? expand_more

We publish quarterly field dispatch reports detailing beneficiary distribution registries (with names masked for dignity), audited receipts, and balance sheets verified by statutory independent chartered accountants.
