## Brand & Style

This design system embodies dignified empowerment, radical empathy, and institutional trustworthiness. Designed for an NGO serving elderly individuals and persons with disabilities, the aesthetic rejects pity-driven design in favor of celebratory agency, clarity, and security.

### Design Movement
**Humanist Modernism with Tactile Accessibility**: Rooted in uncompromising accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AAA standard for text and functional controls), generous scale, and calming organic warmth. It marries crisp architectural clarity with reassuring, physical affordances—deliberate focus indicators, oversized click/touch surfaces, and high-visibility content hierarchies.

### Emotional Target
- **Dignity & Respect**: Celebrating the lives, autonomy, and vibrancy of beneficiaries.
- **Unwavering Trust**: Transparent accountability, certified non-profit security, and immediate validation.
- **Warm Uplift**: Generosity reframed as community solidarity rather than charity.

## Colors

The palette balances institutional integrity with compassionate warmth and life-affirming vitality. Contrast is strictly engineered to surpass WCAG AAA standards across core interactions.

### Role Assignments
- **Primary (`#0F294A` - Deep Trust Navy)**: Anchors global branding, structural navigation, key headlines, and primary button containers. Evokes permanence, safety, and operational credibility.
- **Secondary (`#D97706` / `#E06D53` - Uplifting Amber & Warm Terracotta)**: Used for primary calls-to-action, active donation preset toggles, and conversion accelerators. Delivers an optimistic, human pulse.
- **Tertiary (`#0D9488` / `#ECFDF5` - Resilient Sage & Mint Accent)**: Applied to financial transparency charts, impact metrics, match-funding indicators, and verified security seals.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces**:
  - Deep Slate (`#1E293B`): Body text on light backdrops, ensuring minimum 7:1 contrast.
  - Alabaster Tint (`#F8FAFC`): Base canvas, eliminating harsh glare for light-sensitive or aging eyes.
  - Pearl Border (`#CBD5E1`): High-definition outlines for container clarity without visual noise.

## Typography

The type system prioritizes low-vision legibility, reading fatigue reduction, and high character distinction.

### Type Pairings
- **Headlines (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Friendly, humanist geometry with open apertures and distinct counters. Avoids ambiguous glyph shapes, ensuring fast scanning for older readers.
- **Body & Numerics (`Inter`)**: Engineered for pristine screen clarity. Tall x-height and distinctive shapes prevent character confusion (such as `1`, `l`, and `I`), providing effortless scanning across dense donation disclaimers and impact metrics.

### Sizing Rules
- Body text never falls below 14px; primary explanatory text remains at a generous 18px–20px baseline to assist visually impaired donors.
- Line heights remain at a minimum ratio of 1.5x on body copy to ensure effortless horizontal tracking.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 12-column responsive fluid grid designed around large physical touch points and clear ocular groupings.

### Layout Principles
- **Touch-First Accessibility**: Any actionable interactive element (donation chips, radio buttons, inputs, links) enforces a rigid minimum tap dimension of `48px` (`3rem`), with `56px` (`3.5rem`) preferred for donation amount presets.
- **Content Boundaries**: The main container caps at `1200px` (`75rem`), locking content into optimal reading columns and preventing scattered attention on ultrawide monitors.
- **Responsive Adaptations**:
  - **Mobile (<768px)**: 4-column layout with `20px` margins. Single-column stacked donation forms with sticky bottom action trays.
  - **Tablet (768px–1024px)**: 8-column layout with `32px` margins. Impact stories sit in a 4-column split.
  - **Desktop (>1024px)**: 12-column layout. Split ratio: 7 columns dedicated to narrative validation, live impact feeds, and financial breakdowns; 5 columns reserved for the sticky donation matrix.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids floating or heavily blurred neo-morphic layers in favor of **Crisp Spatial Tiering** and high-contrast ambient boundaries. This ensures low-vision users understand boundaries without relying purely on delicate gradients.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Canvas Surface)**: `#F8FAFC`. The foundational backdrop.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks)**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface with a continuous 1.5px architectural border in `#E2E8F0`. Shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 41, 74, 0.06)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Modules & Sticky Donation Panes)**: `#FFFFFF` surface with a 1px border in `#CBD5E1`. Shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 41, 74, 0.10)`. Provides unmistakable separation from background narrative.
- **Focus & Selection Elevation**: Active elements elevate not through drop-shadow blur, but through an offset `3px` solid high-contrast ring (`#0F294A` or `#D97706`) with an interior `2px` white space gap.

## Shapes

The design uses a balanced rounded visual language (`roundedness: 2`). Corners are smooth and friendly without appearing toy-like or infantilizing.

### Radii Hierarchy
- **Standard Controls (Inputs, Donation Chips, Buttons)**: `8px` (`0.5rem`). Conveys clickable familiarity and crisp boundary definitions.
- **Cards and Modals (`rounded-lg` & `rounded-xl`)**: `16px` to `24px` (`1rem` to `1.5rem`). Softens large modules, framing photography of elders and disabled community members with warm dignity.
- **Donation Badges & Impact Indicators**: Fully rounded pills (`9999px`) to distinguish situational tags, match-multipliers, and status badges from actionable square cards.

## Components

### 1. Donation Amount Chips (Presets)
- **Geometry**: Minimum dimensions `64px` height by `100%` column width.
- **State Styling**:
  - *Inactive*: `#FFFFFF` surface, `2px` solid `#CBD5E1` border, `#1E293B` bold type.
  - *Active/Selected*: `#ECFDF5` surface (or light warm amber), `2.5px` solid `#0D9488` border, bold headline text, accented with a top-right verification checkmark.
  - *Focus*: `3px` focus ring with a `2px` offset.

### 2. Primary Action Buttons
- **Geometry**: Large `56px` height, full width across mobile and form containers.
- **Visuals**: Primary donation trigger uses Warm Amber/Terracotta (`#D97706` / `#E06D53`) with crisp `#FFFFFF` label text.
- **Hover/Active**: Darkens by 8% with a slight 1px inward pressing sensation; never relies on color change alone—includes an accompanying directional chevron icon.

### 3. Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Hit Area**: 48x48px wrapper bounding box with a 24x24px visible glyph box.
- **Visuals**: High-contrast checkmarks; selected state filled with Navy `#0F294A` featuring a bold white check or radial dot. Avoids faint low-contrast borders.

### 4. Input Fields (Custom Gift, Donor Details)
- **Geometry**: Minimum height `54px`, `18px` font size to prevent automatic zooming on iOS Safari.
- **Labels & Hints**: Persistent floating or top-aligned labels (never placeholder-only). High-contrast label color (`#0F294A`).
- **Currency Affix**: Large, unyielding currency symbol locked to the left in `#64748B`.

### 5. Verified Trust Badges & Match Alerts
- **Impact & Match Pill**: Emerald/Sage styling (`#ECFDF5` background, `#0D9488` border and icon) prominently stating impact (e.g., "Matched 2x by Community Partner").
- **Security & Non-Profit Badges**: Horizontal trust strip showing 501(c)(3) registration, SSL encryption, and high Charity Navigator ratings using authentic dual-tone iconography.

### 6. Story & Impact Cards
- **Structure**: Uncluttered, vertical rhythm. Authentic high-resolution imagery showcasing elderly and disabled participants actively shaping their communities, paired with direct quotes set in `body-xl` and transparent fund-allocation charts.
