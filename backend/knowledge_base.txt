CORE PROGRAMS & FIELD DATA:
1. Mobility Aids: Ergonomic walkers, quad-base walkers, heavy-duty wheelchairs. Average cost of an orthopedic walker is ₹1,500. It prevents bedsores and gives elders independence.
2. Palliative Care: Doorstep medical assistance, physiotherapy, and chronic illness medications for bedridden seniors. Assigned Geriatric Therapists like Dr. Ananya Sen conduct bi-weekly visits.
3. Nutrition Support: Daily hot meals, high-protein supplements, and essential grocery rations.
- Scale: 14,200+ Elders Supported, 50+ Active Field Clinics.
